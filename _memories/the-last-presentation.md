Final presentation. The one we'd rehearsed eleven times, in a hall, in front of
a panel who were visibly counting minutes.

Dev's laptop was held together with tape on two corners. This was not a surprise
to anyone. It was, however, a surprise at the exact moment the charger didn't
fully engage and the battery started counting down from 4%.

What happened next is the single most chaotic thirty seconds of my time at
ATSMU. Ishita took over the narration. Ananya turned to face the panel so
nobody else had to watch the screen. Fatima started talking over the slide
change, loudly and completely naturally, as if this had always been the plan.

It went up as a pass. With a comment about "presenter confidence under
technical difficulty", which everyone in the room agreed was fair.

Afterwards nobody went straight back to the room. We ended up in the canteen
for about three hours, talking about everything except the presentation, which
is what we always did.
