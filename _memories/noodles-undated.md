No date. No faces we can put names to. Just a stall near the north gate, a
shutter that's been down for years now, and six people who were extremely happy
about noodles.

This photo turned up in a phone backup in 2024 with no folder name, no
timestamp anyone trusted, and the caption: **"the good one"**.

It's here because that's the whole point of this site. Not every memory needs
a caption and a verified date. Some of them just need to not get deleted.

If you were there — [claim it](/about/#add). If you know what stall this was,
the comments are open.
