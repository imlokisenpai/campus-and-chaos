Nobody knew what to do with themselves. That's the honest summary.

We turned up with suitcases that were heavier than anything we actually owned,
a folder of documents that was definitely the wrong folder, and the firm
collective belief that the hostel Wi-Fi would be fast enough to matter.

It was not.

For about three hours we all pretended to be unpacking. Then Kabir found the
kitchen, and half the batch followed him, and that was effectively the first
group chat. Nobody asked anyone to. It just happened.

> The first afternoon is the one I'd genuinely give back. Nobody had
> performances to give yet. You were just a lot of people standing around
> going, "so... this is it?"

The sunset photo was taken from the third-floor balcony at 9:04pm, by the light
of somebody's phone torch, because no camera had been unpacked yet.
