There was never a rota. Somebody would be unable to sleep around 1am, come
down, and there would already be two people there. Sometimes four.

No phones out. That was the actual rule, and it was the only rule. If you'd
turned up hoping to sleep, you were in the wrong place.

Ananya maintained for years that she was always there because of an assignment.
She was not always there because of an assignment.

The lit-lamp photo is the best one anyone took in that canteen and it was
taken at 2:07am by someone who should have been paying attention to their
degree.

If you were there: [add it](/about/#add). Somebody in this batch remembers it
better than we do.
