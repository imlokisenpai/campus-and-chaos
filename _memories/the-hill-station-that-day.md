We left at 5:40am. Twenty minutes late, which in this batch counted as
punctual.

Twelve people, four two-wheelers, one car, and a route plan that consisted of
"take that road, then someone will know". Ananya turned out to be that
someone. We followed her for six hours without questioning it, which was
either trust or a collective failure of judgement.

The chai stall at the top was run by a man who had clearly seen everything
before. He looked at twelve exhausted students, at one helmet, and at Yash
still holding the selfie stick, and he said only: *"Tea or coffee?"*

We said tea. We all said tea. Forty minutes later the hill didn't feel very far
away anymore.

There are four versions of the ride back and everyone remembers a different
one. The 11pm version involves a police checkpoint that I have never been able
to get a coherent account of.
