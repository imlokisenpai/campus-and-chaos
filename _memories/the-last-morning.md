We were supposed to gather at seven.

At 6:20am, a group chat message went up that just said *"who's awake"*, and
forty-one people replied with a thumbs up, which at that hour is a fairly
ambiguous emotional state.

There was no ceremony yet. That was later, and it was fine. The bit that
actually mattered happened in a corridor for about twenty minutes while people
pretended they weren't taking photos of each other.

About four hundred photos exist from that morning. Roughly eleven of them are
any good. This page is mostly about the other three hundred and eighty-nine.

The one nobody planned: at 6:52am, someone started singing, badly, and then
about thirty people joined in, in a corridor, at volume, at 6:52 in the morning,
while two faculty members walked past and did not say anything.

That's the version I'd keep.
