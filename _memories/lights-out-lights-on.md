Power cut at 11pm. Within four minutes, somebody on the floor below had found
a torch, somebody else had found the good speaker, and by 11:20 we were all on
the roof.

Nobody planned it. Nobody would admit to starting it, which is the only rule we
ever agreed on for that roof.

Dev filmed most of it. Dev also filmed himself singing, which was a choice he
made repeatedly and at volume.

The bulb in the corridor photo had been flickering for about a week at that
point. It died properly two days later, and nobody replaced it, so for the rest
of the year that corridor just had a shadow in it. Arguably an improvement.

**The video is forty seconds long on purpose.** The original was eleven
minutes and consisted mostly of the same four people.
