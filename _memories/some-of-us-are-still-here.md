Eighteen months after we left, seven of us got into the same room.

This was not easy to arrange and it happened almost by accident, which is how
most things in this batch happen. A birthday, then a week nobody could get off
work, then one of us just booked the train.

The thing nobody said out loud, and which everyone noticed: it took a full
two days before anybody asked anybody a serious question. Two days of food and
noise and old arguments, and then somewhere around hour thirty the conversation
turned, and it turned hard, because the people who'd watched each other
pretend to be fine for four years finally had room to say so.

We did the important version of this, and then we did the stupid version of
this, and the stupid version went on until 4am.

Most of us are now in four different cities. The group chat is louder than
ever, which is the least predictable outcome of all of this.

**And then in second year somebody said something that never got resolved, and
it's still unresolved. Somebody tell me what happened.**
